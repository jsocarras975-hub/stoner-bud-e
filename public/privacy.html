Privacy Policy — Stoner Bud-E

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · © 2026 N3xUs Konc3pt'z

Stoner Bud-E is a 21+ cannabis companion app. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

What we collect

Account information. If you create an account, we store your email address and a securely hashed password so you can sign in. You may change your email or password in the app. We do not verify your email address.

Your content. If you sign in, the app syncs your journal entries, stash jars, saved chats, playlists, and settings to our server so they are available across your devices. This content is stored in our database and is only visible to you, except for content you choose to share (see below).

Shared content. If you post on the Group Sesh community board, write a strain review, or comment, that content (and the display name you choose) is visible to other users of the app. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Chat and voice. When you chat with the AI buddy or use voice chat, your messages and voice recordings are sent to our AI providers (Groq, Google Gemini, and OpenRouter) to generate replies and transcriptions. Photos you share for analysis are sent to those providers for that purpose only. We do not sell your messages, recordings, or photos.

Music. The in-app music player uses the Audius API to search and stream songs. Your music searches and listening are handled by Audius under their own policy.

Payments. Premium is sold through redeem codes, Cash App, or Coinbase Commerce. We do not store your card or bank details. Cash App and Coinbase handle payments under their own policies.

On-device data. Your age-gate status (21+ only), app lock PIN, keyboard learned words, and notification preferences are stored only on your device and are never sent to our server.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or your device advertising ID. We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not show third-party ads.

How we use your information

We use your information to run the app: to sign you in, sync your content across devices, generate AI replies, send password-reset emails (only when you ask for one), and to verify premium payments. We do not use your information for advertising.

Your choices

You can export your data as JSON from Settings, clear your chats or journal, and delete your account from the Account screen. Deleting your account removes your account and your synced content from our server. Content you posted publicly (Group Sesh posts, strain reviews) may remain visible in anonymized form after deletion. You can also use most of the app without signing in; your journal, stash, and settings then stay only on your device.

Children and age

Stoner Bud-E is for adults 21 and older only. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you are under 21, do not use the app.

Data security and retention

We store your data on secure servers and use encrypted connections. We keep your account and synced content until you delete your account. Backups may retain copies for a short time after deletion.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for material changes, let you know in the app.
